#### Yoga Teacher Traning Course
An enriching month-long immersion into yoga practice and philosophy. This TTC guided students in developing a strong, injury-free self-practice and prepared them to teach with confidence and clarity. Mornings began with pranayama and chanting, followed by asana, meditation sessions, and integral yoga theory lessons: all in Alaya’s peaceful natural setting.

May 2024, September 2024, May 2025, December 2025

#### Ayurvedic Cooking Workshop
This series of 3-day workshops introduced participants to the principles of Ayurveda, focusing on personalized nutrition using local, affordable ingredients. The sessions included both theory and hands-on cooking, aiming to reconnect people with the healing potential of food. Delivered in English and Indonesian, the workshops supported local knowledge-sharing through scholarships.

3 batches in August 2024

The training took place four times over 3 days in 2024, with 4 - 6 participants including international students and local students. They were all level 1 trainings, giving a solid introduction to basic massage including techniques from Thai Yoga, Ayurveda and Siddha medecine.

The students were given a book to keep practicing until they would be ready for level 2.
