## Section 2 — Intro

[H2, script font] Your Stay,the Natural Way

Whether you're seeking solitude in a cozy private cottage or planning a group retreat, Alaya offers restful accommodation in harmony with nature.

Our eco-friendly lodgings are designed for deep rest and connection. Each space is crafted with care—merging rustic charm and modern essentials, surrounded by gardens, trees, and birdsong. From traditional wooden houses to open-air lodges, every detail honors the land and minimizes impact.

Wake up to the sounds of nature. Breathe in fresh mountain air. Stay gently, tread lightly.

Alaya Ecolodge wants to celebrate the local culture and produce of the bioregion, so we have named our cottages after the side dishes of a “Tumpeng” which is a popular Indonesian celebration food.
